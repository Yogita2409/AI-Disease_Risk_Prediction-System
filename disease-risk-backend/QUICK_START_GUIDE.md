## 🎯 How to Use

### Step 1: Create an Account
1. Click **"Sign Up"** in the navigation bar
2. Enter your name, email, and password
3. Click **"Create Account"**
4. You'll be automatically logged in

### Step 2: Make a Prediction
1. Click **"Predict Disease"** in the navigation
2. Fill in your health parameters:
   - Age
   - Gender
   - Blood Pressure (mmHg)
   - Glucose Level (mg/dL)
   - Cholesterol (mg/dL)
   - Symptoms (optional)
3. Click **"Predict Risk"**
4. View your results with:
   - Risk Level (High/Moderate/Low)
   - Probability Score
   - Model Breakdown (3 ML algorithms)
   - Health Recommendations

### Step 3: Download Your Report
- Click **"Download PDF Report"** button
- Save the professional health report

### Step 4: View Your Dashboard
1. Click **"Dashboard"** in navigation
2. See:
   - Total predictions count
   - Risk distribution (High/Moderate/Low)
   - Interactive charts (Pie & Bar)
   - Recent predictions table

### Step 5: Check Your History
1. Click **"History"** in navigation
2. View all past predictions
3. Download any previous report

## 📊 Understanding the Results

### Risk Levels:
- **High Risk (≥60%)**: Immediate medical consultation recommended
- **Moderate Risk (35-60%)**: Regular monitoring and lifestyle changes advised
- **Low Risk (<35%)**: Maintain healthy habits

### Model Breakdown:
The system uses 3 ML algorithms:
1. **Logistic Regression** - Statistical probability model
2. **Random Forest** - Decision tree ensemble
3. **Support Vector Machine** - Pattern recognition

The final prediction is an ensemble (combination) of all three models.
